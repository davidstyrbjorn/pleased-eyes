What's it like to be inside a nuclear reactor?
What if your tasked with defending the nuclear fuel from incoming particles?
Only to allow certain particles to collide with the fuel to split it creating energy

Types of "enemy" particles
- Turret
    Goes to a set radius from the core and starts shooting at an even predictable interval

- Straight shooter
    Moves into the core, damaging it if it reaches

- Glitch
    Moves similar to straight shooter but jerks left/right making it harder to predict and hit

- Zombie
    If it reaches the reactor barrier it attaches itself to the barrier and starts moving towards the player
